Table 
    Border
    align - will change table position (left , center, right)
    bgcolor - change background color of table
    width , height
    cellspacing -- space around the cells (default : 1)
    cellpadding -- space inside the cells (default : 1)

    align - data gets shifted (left , center , right)
    bgcolor - change background color of row
    height
    
td/th
    align - data gets shifted (left , center , right)
    bgcolor - change background color of data
    width , height
    colspan -- merging columns
    rowspan -- merging rows
